## Trust Boundaries

| T-01-DUP | Tampering | raw_observations duplicate rows | high | mitigate | UNIQUE(country,year,indicator) constraint + pre-insert assert double-check (D-11, belt-and-suspenders) |
| T-01-PANEL | Tampering | panel drift from hand edits | medium | mitigate | panel is always rebuilt with if_exists='replace' from raw_observations; never edited in place (D-12) |
</threat_model>
